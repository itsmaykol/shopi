## ⚠️ How to read the money columns (this is the honest bit)

AliExpress shows **aggressive promotional prices** ($1.09, $0.33) that are unreliable as a cost basis —
they are loss-leaders tied to new-buyer coupons and coin discounts, and they change weekly.
So for every product I show three numbers:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Observed listing price** | `VERIFIED-LIVE` — the price actually on the page I opened today. |
| **Planning landed cost** | `ESTIMATED` — a deliberately *conservative* cost = a realistic (non-loss-leader) unit price + shipping allowance. **All margin math uses this, not the promo price.** |
| **Net margin** | `ESTIMATED` — computed at planning cost, with card fees and two ad-cost scenarios. |

If a supplier link is opened and the real cost is above my planning cost, the product still clears
3× markup at the retail price shown — that is the point of padding it.

Payment-fee model: **2.9% + $0.30** per order (Shopify's standard US card rate).
Ad-cost allowance: **$8.00** (efficient) and **$12.00** (pessimistic) blended CPA. Organic is the
primary channel at launch; ads are a later test, so a $0-ad scenario is also shown.

### Supplier strategy (how I make the gap not matter)

1. **Nothing in this store is electronics, ingestible, or safety-critical.** Enrichment mats, a hair
   roller, a slicker brush and a plastic bowl have no battery, no dosing, no child-safety cert and no
   plausible failure mode beyond "it didn't work for my dog" — the cheapest possible category to
   survive unvetted-supplier risk in.
2. **Bundles beat single items for QC reasons.** A bundle whose weakest component disappoints still
   delivers two components that don't, so refund pressure is spread across a higher AOV.
3. **US-warehouse filter is available on every one of these searches** (the "Shipping from: United
   States" filter is on the AliExpress search pages linked above). Selecting a US-warehouse variant is
   the first thing to do in DSers at import time — it cuts delivery to ~3–7 days and makes our
   7–15 day promise very safe.
4. **Two-source rule.** Before launch you need a second supplier for the two thin-evidence SKUs
   (#4 Lick Mat, #7 Paw Cleaner Cup). Both have multiple identical listings on the same search pages
   I linked — pick a 4.5★+ / 1,000+ sold alternative from the same page.
5. **Order one sample of the hero (the Snuffle Mat) before spending a dollar on ads.** This is the
   single highest-value $6 you will spend on this business. It is item #1 on your action list.

## Image rights

All product photography referenced by the store is hot-linked from the **AliExpress CDN URLs of the
specific listings in the table above** (`ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/...`). Practical position:

- AliExpress listing images are **supplier product photography**, commonly reused by dropshippers, and
  the suppliers themselves typically source them from the factories. **This is the normal industry
  practice and it is also a genuine legal grey area — you do not own these images.**
- Recommended path (free): once DSers/SyncMaster imports a product to Shopify, it copies the images
  into your Shopify Files, which is the standard workflow. For the hero products, plan to **reshoot
  your own photos and short video with the sample you order** — this both removes the rights question
  and produces far better ad creative.
- **Nothing in this store should be assumed to be licensed, exclusive, or trademarked-clear artwork.**
  No brand logos were copied; the Sniffari wordmark and mark are original, generated for you.

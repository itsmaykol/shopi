## D. Method note on how the numbers were treated

1. **Promotional prices were never used for margin math.** AliExpress shows loss-leader prices ($0.33,
   $1.09) that are tied to new-buyer coupons and change weekly. Every margin in `PRODUCT-TABLE.md` uses a
   deliberately padded "planning landed cost" instead, so the business survives a worst-case price rise.
2. **Delivery windows were padded, not optimised.** The fastest observed window was 3 days; the store
   promises 7–15. Under-promising is worth more than the conversion it costs.
3. **Conflicting sources are recorded, not averaged.** Where two publishers gave wildly different hashtag
   view counts, both are listed above.

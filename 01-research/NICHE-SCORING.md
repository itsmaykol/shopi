## Scoring model

Eight criteria, 1–10 (10 = best), weighted because they don't matter equally to a zero-budget,
mobile-first, organic-video store:

| Criterion | Weight | Why that weight |
|---|---|---|
| Demand & trend direction (growing, not peaking) | ×1.25 | A growing category forgives mistakes |
| Margin potential | ×1.25 | Thin margins kill dropshipping stores |
| TikTok/Reels virality | ×1.25 | Zero ad budget = organic video is the only traffic engine |
| Competition saturation (**10 = wide open**) | ×1.00 | Inverted: scores high when it's *easy* to stand out |
| Repeat purchase / upsell | ×1.00 | LTV without paid acquisition |
| Low return risk | ×1.00 | Returns erase dropship margin |
| Shipping ease (small/light/durable) | ×0.75 | Affects cost + damage rate |
| Emotional buying drive | ×0.75 | Drives impulse purchase |

Weighted score = Σ(score × weight) ÷ 8.25 → normalised to 10.

## Evidence behind the scores

### Pet — the winner's evidence
- **U.S. pet industry: $158B (2025) → $165B projected (2026), +4.4%**, of which ~2 pts is inflation — so real growth continues. Supplies/live animals/OTC: $34.4B → $34.6B. `VERIFIED-CITED` — APPA via https://petsplusmag.com/u-s-pet-industry-reaches-158-billion-in-2025-poised-for-continued-growth-in-2026/ and https://companieshistory.com/pet-care-market-statistics/
- **95 million U.S. households own at least one pet.** `VERIFIED-CITED` — same APPA coverage.
- **30% of dog owners bought a food-dispensing toy in 2024; 22% of cat owners did.** Enrichment is described as the shift "from fun to function," with cat indoor/solo-play growing fastest. `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://globalpetindustry.com/news/function-over-fun-interactive-pet-toys-shift-toward-enrichment/
- **Seasonality lands perfectly on our launch:** "dog toys" search interest runs 42–83 and **peaks in November and December**; "interactive pet toys" peak in December; chew toys peak in November. `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://www.accio.com/business/trends2025dog and https://www.accio.com/business/trending-pet-toys
- **Q4 gifting is huge and quantified:** 81% of surveyed U.S. pet owners buy holiday gifts for their pets; the single most common spend band is **$51–75 (15% of respondents)**; 11% spend $126–150; toys 58%, comfort gifts 46%. `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://www.usatoday.com/money/blueprint/pet-insurance/christmas-gifts-for-dogs/
- **Why pet loses points on saturation (4/10):** commodity pet accessories are heavily advertised and Amazon-price-anchored. The plan counters this by *not selling commodity toys* — the store is positioned on indoor enrichment + shedding/paw cleanliness, and bundles, rather than racing on single-item price.

### Home organization & cleaning — strong, but second
- Household cleaning tools & supplies: **$34.3B (2025) → $36.1B (2026), 5.4% CAGR to $57.9B by 2035.** `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/household-cleaning-tools-and-supplies-market
- #CleanTok scale: TikTok itself put cumulative **#CleanTok views above 78B**; other reporting cites 150B+; **54% of users bought a household product after seeing it on TikTok, 71% of those unplanned.** `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://www.marketingdive.com/news/unilever-tiktok-cleaning-clean-tok/653463/ , https://rozie.app/cleantok-viral-cleaning-services/
- **Why it lost to pet:** repeat purchase is structurally weak (a good scrubber lasts years, so no consumable loop), paid-ad saturation on the exact hero items is extreme (spin scrubbers, cleaning gel, The Pink Stuff), and emotional drive is low. It scored 7.03 vs pet's 8.36.

### Beauty — highest margin, disqualified on risk
- Beauty & personal care is **22.5% of TikTok Shop GMV ($2.49B US)**; skincare 36% of that; fragrance converts ~8%. `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://www.accio.com/wow/business-best-selling-products-on-tiktok-shop.html , https://www.quicksync.pro/blog/tiktok-shop-trending-products-2026/ (Charm.io data)
- It still lost: Q2-2026 beauty data shows the winners are **branded** Korean/derm brands (Medicube, Dr. Melaxin took 22.9% of the category), which we cannot legally sell; and skin devices invite health/result claims the brief forbids. Scores 7.24.

### Car — disqualified on seasonality
- Car upholstery cleaner category: **$455M Sept → $440M Oct → $420M Nov → $372M Dec** projected, recovering in spring. `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://simporter.com/trends/car-upholstery-cleaner/
- Launching a car-detailing store on 2026-10-02 means launching into a 4-month demand decline. Scores 6.24.

### Jewelry — best margins, wrong moment
- Beauty/jewelry margin bands 50–70% and the best shipping profile of any niche. `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://www.autods.com/blog/shopify-niches/ , https://branvas.com/blogs/news/high-ticket-dropshipping-niches
- Lost because demand is flat-to-soft (score 6 on trend), trust barrier for an unknown brand is the highest of any niche, and Q4 jewelry traffic is dominated by brands running paid ads we won't have. Scores 7.09.

### Baby — disqualified on safety/liability
- Fast-growing (7–8% annually, 70%+ of parents discover products on social). `VERIFIED-CITED` — https://www.fforder.com/post/9profitabledropshippingnichesfor2026-longtermgrowth
- The hard constraint says no baby products without safety standards — an unbranded dropship supplier cannot demonstrate CPSC compliance. Excluded as a winner on purpose, not on numbers. Scores 6.85.

## 🏆 Winner: **Indoor pet enrichment & everyday care (dogs + cats)**

**Three-sentence justification:**
1. It is the only niche that scores 9–10 on both *emotional buying drive* and *repeat purchase* while showing **verified growth (+4.4% to $165B in 2026) and a demand curve that peaks exactly in our November–December launch window** ("dog toys" search interest peaks Nov–Dec; 81% of U.S. pet owners buy holiday gifts for their pets, with $51–75 the most common spend band).
2. Margins are structurally excellent — the exact items I verified cost **$2.74–$7.39 landed** and retail at $19.99–$34.99 (4–8× markup), with small, light, durable, non-electronic goods that ship cheaply and rarely come back.
3. Its one weakness (commodity saturation) is a *positioning* problem, not a market problem: I will stand the store on **indoor enrichment + shedding/paw cleanliness bundles** rather than competing on single commodity toys, which is also the content angle that performs best on short-form video.

**Sub-niche positioning statement:** *"Enrichment and everyday cleanup for indoor dogs and cats — the stuff that keeps a bored pet from redecorating your house."*

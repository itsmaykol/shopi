Frequently asked questions

Short answers, no small print games — including the ones that cost us sales.

Ordering & payment

What payment methods do you take?
Card payments and the usual wallets are handled by our checkout provider. We never see or store your card details.

Do you charge sales tax?
Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address, wherever it applies.

Can I change or cancel my order?
If it has not been dispatched yet, yes — email us with your order number as soon as you can.

Is there a discount code?
New customers get 10% off for joining the email list. Bundles are already discounted 12–17%.

Shipping

How long will my order take?
Most US orders arrive in 7–15 days. Every order gets tracking by email when it dispatches.

Why does it take longer than Amazon?
We do not hold stock. Your item is packed and shipped by our supplier partner, which keeps prices low and means we never charge you for guessed-at inventory.

What does shipping cost?
Free over $35. Below that it is a flat $4.95 in the US.

My tracking has not updated.
That is normal on international legs — tracking often pauses between handover points. If it has been over 15 business days with no movement, contact us.

Returns & problems

What if it arrives damaged or wrong?
Send a photo with your order number within 7 days. We replace or refund in full, including shipping.

What if my pet just does not like it?
Return it unused and unwashed within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price.

How long do refunds take?
We process refunds within 2 business days; your bank then takes 5–10 business days to post it.

The products

Are any of these safe to leave with a pet unattended?
No. Supervise, then put them away. They are feeding tools, not chew toys, and none are indestructible.

Do any products need batteries or an app?
No. No electronics, no subscriptions, ever.

Are these vet-approved or do they treat anything?
No, and we will never claim that. They are enrichment and grooming accessories. For a diagnosed condition, talk to your vet.

How do I clean them?
Exact care instructions are in the specs table on each product page.

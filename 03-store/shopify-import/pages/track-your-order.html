Typical timeline for a US order

Day 0Order placed, confirmation email sent
Day 1–3Packed and submitted to the carrier
Day 3–15In transit, tracking updates at handover points

During the last week of November and the first three weeks of December, add about 5–7 days. Peak season is genuinely slower everywhere, and it is better to plan for it.

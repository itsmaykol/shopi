Frequently asked questions

Short answers, no small print games. Including the ones that cost us sales.

Ordering & payment

What payment methods do you take?

Card payments and the usual wallets are handled by our checkout provider. We never see or store your card details. Available methods are shown at checkout based on your country.

Do you charge sales tax?

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address, wherever it applies. The price you see on the product page is before tax and shipping.

Can I change or cancel my order?

If the order has not been dispatched yet, yes — email us with your order number as soon as you can. Once it is with the carrier we cannot recall it, but you can use the returns process when it arrives.

Is there a discount code?

New customers get 10% off for signing up to the email list — the code arrives instantly. Bundles are already discounted 12–17% and stack on top.

Shipping

How long will my order take?

Most US orders arrive in 7–15 days. Every order gets tracking by email when it dispatches, and you can check status on the track your order page.

Why does it take longer than Amazon?

We do not hold stock. Your item is packed and shipped by our supplier partner, which is what keeps prices low and means we never charge you for inventory we are guessing at.

Do you ship outside the US?

The store is set up for US delivery at launch. International options are being added — contact us and we will tell you honestly whether we can ship to you yet.

What does shipping cost?

Free over $35. Below that it is a flat $4.95 in the US.

My tracking has not updated in a few days.

That is normal on international legs — tracking often pauses between handover points. If it has been more than 15 business days with no movement, contact us and we will chase it, then refund or reship under the refund policy.

Returns & problems

What if it arrives damaged or wrong?

Send a photo with your order number within 7 days. We will replace it or refund it in full, including shipping — you do not pay return postage when the fault is ours.

What if my pet just does not like it?

Return it unused and unwashed within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. See the refund policy for who pays return postage.

How long do refunds take?

We process refunds within 2 business days of receiving the return or approving the claim. Your bank then takes 5–10 business days to post it.

The products

Are any of these safe to leave with a pet unattended?

No. Supervise, then put them away. Snuffle mats, lick mats and slow feeders are feeding tools, not chew toys, and none of them are indestructible.

Do any products need batteries or an app?

No. There is no electronic device in this store and there never will be a subscription.

Are these vet-approved or do they treat anything?

No, and we will never claim that. They are enrichment and grooming accessories. For a diagnosed condition or a serious behaviour problem, talk to your vet or a qualified trainer.

How do I clean them?

Each product page has the exact care instructions in the specs table. As a rule: bowl and lick mat are dishwasher safe, snuffle mat is machine washable and air dry, brushes and rollers just need emptying and a wipe.

Still stuck?

Contact us with your order number and we will sort it.

How long does shipping take?

Most US orders arrive in 7–15 days. Items ship directly from our supplier partners, which is how we keep prices where they are. You get tracking by email as soon as it is dispatched, and you can check status any time on the track your order page.

Why is it not 2-day shipping like a big store?

Because we are not stocking a warehouse. Two-day shipping would mean buying inventory up front and charging you for it. We would rather be honest about a 7–15 day window than promise two days and miss it — which is the single most common complaint about stores like this one.

What if my pet doesn't like it?

Send it back unused within 30 days of delivery and we refund the item price. Full details, including who pays return postage, are in the refund policy.

Are these safe for a puppy or a chewer?

Nothing here is a chew toy. Snuffle mats and lick mats are feeding tools and should be used supervised, then put away. If your pet destroys fabric or silicone, take it away and try again when they are calmer — swallowed fabric or silicone is a vet visit.

Do you make health claims?

No. Nothing here treats, cures or prevents anything. These are enrichment and grooming accessories. If your pet has a diagnosed condition or a behaviour problem, that is a conversation with your vet or a qualified trainer, not a shopping cart.

# Sniffari — Viral Launch Kit

**Products covered:** the two heroes — **Snuffle Mat ($34.99)** and **Fur Lifter ($24.99)**
**Format:** TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts (same vertical edit works on all three)
**Rule this whole kit follows:** sell the *owner's* problem, never a health claim. No "cures anxiety",
no "vet approved", no fake results. The products are enrichment and grooming gear, and the copy says so.

---

## 0. Read this before you film anything

Three things separate accounts that sell from accounts that get views:

1. **The first 2 seconds are the whole video.** Every script below opens on the problem, the mess, or the
   satisfying half of the action. Never open on a logo, a title card, or you talking to camera.
2. **Show the product doing the thing by second 5.** Vertical, close, hands in frame, no music-video
   montage. The demo *is* the ad.
3. **One idea per video.** If a clip needs a sentence to explain it, it is two videos.

**What you need to shoot all of this:** one phone, a window, and the actual product in your hand.
Everything below is designed to be filmed in a single afternoon once your sample order arrives — which is
exactly why ordering the sample is item #1 on your action list.

> ⚠️ **Legal/ethical note that matters commercially:** do not fabricate outcomes, do not use footage of a
> pet that is not being cared for normally, and do not claim the product treats a behaviour or health
> problem. Beyond being your rule, both TikTok and Meta will demote or ban accounts for it, and "this
> fixed my dog's anxiety" is a health claim that can attract FTC attention in the US.

# 1. HERO PRODUCT A — Snuffle Mat ($34.99)

## 1.1 Three TikTok / Reels scripts

### Script A1 — "90 seconds" (the problem-first workhorse)
*Best performing structure for this product. Film this one first.*

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Voiceover / audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5s | **Close-up, floor level.** Dog's head goes down into a bowl and the kibble is gone in one continuous motion. | `Dinner: 90 seconds.` | No VO. Use a fast, percussive trending sound, or the sound of the bowl scraping. |
| 1.5–3.5s | Cut to the **same dog pacing a hallway**, looking at the camera, whining once. | `Which is why he's bored by 6:05pm.` | Silence here — the drop in music does the work. |
| 3.5–8s | **Hands load the snuffle mat**: kibble scattered into the folds. Overhead, tight, satisfying. | `So we put dinner inside this instead.` | Natural sound: kibble hitting fabric. |
| 8–13s | **Dog works the mat.** Timelapse at 2× or 3×, snout buried, tail going. Show at least 8 real seconds of work. | `Now it takes 12 minutes.` | Trending upbeat audio returns. |
| 13–17s | Dog **flops down** next to the mat, tired and calm. | `Same food. Same dog. Different evening.` | Music settles. |
| 17–20s | Product on the floor, hand patting it once. | `Snuffle Mat — $34.99 · free shipping over $35` | VO (optional, casual): *"Link's in the bio if your dog inhales dinner too."* |

**Caption:** Dinner used to take 90 seconds. Now it takes 12 minutes, and he sleeps instead of pacing the hallway. 🐾 #dogsoftiktok #enrichment #dogmom

### Script A2 — "Objection killer" (builds trust, converts the hesitant buyer)
*This is the one that sells to the person who has seen five snuffle mats and bought none.*

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2s | You holding the mat up, then **shaking it** to show it is floppy fabric, not a box. | `"He'll just flip it over."` | VO: *"Everyone says their dog will just flip it over."* |
| 2–5s | Set it on tile, press the base with one foot, show it does not slide. | `Non-slip base.` | VO: *"It doesn't slide. Try it."* |
| 5–9s | Put a **shallow scatter** across the top, let the dog eat easily. | `Day 1: easy mode.` | VO: *"Day one, you make it easy so they win."* |
| 9–13s | **Then** load the folds properly and let the dog dig. | `Day 4: full meal.` | VO: *"By day four, dinner is buried and takes twelve minutes."* |
| 13–17s | Pull the mat out and **shake the crumbs off**, then into the washing machine. | `Also: machine washable.` | VO: *"And it goes in the wash, so it doesn't become the smelliest thing you own."* |
| 17–20s | Mat on the floor, dog already lying on it. | `Snuffle Mat — $34.99` | VO: *"Link's in the bio."* |

**Caption:** "He'll just flip it over" — no he won't, and here's day 1 vs day 4 so you don't mess up the introduction. #dogtraining #enrichment #puppytips

## 1.2 Five ad hooks (first 2 seconds, verbatim — swap between scripts freely)

1. **"If your dog eats in under two minutes, this is the problem."** *(problem callout — highest cold-traffic CTR)*
2. **"Dinner used to take 90 seconds. Now it takes twelve minutes."** *(before/after — best for warm audiences)*
3. **"Three days of rain and no walk. Watch what this does."** *(seasonal, strongest Nov–Feb)*
4. **"He's not bored because he needs more toys. He's bored because he needs a job."** *(reframe — best comment section)*
5. **"Put dinner inside this instead of a bowl."** *(instructional — lowest cost per click, best for Retargeting)*

## 1.3 Shot list (one filming session, ~40 minutes)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dog eating from a bowl, floor level, close | Highest frame rate your phone allows; the speed is the point |
| 2 | The empty bowl, one beat | Gives you an edit point |
| 3 | Dog pacing / staring at you | Get 15 seconds of it; you will only use 3 |
| 4 | Hands loading the mat, overhead | Shoot **twice**: a shallow scatter and a full load |
| 5 | Dog working the mat, nose-level and overhead | Get at least 60 seconds of raw footage |
| 6 | Timelapse of the full working session | Lock the phone on a tripod/stack of books |
| 7 | Dog flopping down afterwards | The emotional payoff — do not skip it |
| 8 | Shaking crumbs off the mat | Satisfying; also communicates "washable" |
| 9 | Mat going into the washing machine | Five seconds, proves the cleaning claim |
| 10 | Clean product shot on the floor | Your outro frame for every video |
| 11 | **Close-up of the weave/folds** in daylight | Makes the product look like something, not a rag |
| 12 | The dog sniffing **before** the food goes in | Free B-roll you will reuse constantly |

# 4. Three ad angles to test (do NOT run these until the store has organic proof)

Test only after you have **at least 10 organic posts with real engagement** and **one real customer review**.
Until then, ads are a tax on learning. Also note: you are on a zero-spend plan, so every bullet below is a
plan for a later date, not an instruction to spend today.

### Angle 1 — "Problem callout" (cold traffic)
- **Hook:** *"If your dog eats in under two minutes, this is the problem."*
- **Creative:** Script A1, trimmed to 15s, hard cut on the first frame.
- **Audience:** Broad, US, 25–55, all genders, interests: dogs, pet supplies, dog training.
  Let the algorithm find the buyer — do not over-stack interests.
- **Objective:** Sales, lowest-cost conversions.
- **Success metric:** cost per add-to-cart under ~$8, cost per purchase under ~$18.
- **Why test it:** cleanest signal on whether the product has cold demand at all.

### Angle 2 — "Satisfying demo, no words" (cold + retargeting)
- **Hook:** *"This is one pass on one couch cushion."*
- **Creative:** Script B1 with **no voiceover at all** — on-screen text only, original sound.
- **Audience (cold):** Broad, US, 25–60, interests: cleaning, home, pets. Second ad set with
  **Retargeting: everyone who viewed a product page in 7 days, excluding purchasers.**
- **Objective:** Sales.
- **Success metric:** thumb-stop rate above 25%, cost per purchase under ~$15 (this product is cheaper).
- **Why test it:** sound-off silent video is the format most likely to also work when repurposed to
  Pinterest and YouTube Shorts later.

### Angle 3 — "Bundle / AOV lift" (warm + cart abandoners)
- **Hook:** *"Two things for a dog who is bored and sheds."*
- **Creative:** 20s pairing of Snuffle Mat + Fur Lifter, bundle price on screen, free-shipping bar visible.
- **Audience:** **Retargeting only** — viewed product page 14 days, added to cart 7 days, and past
  newsletter subscribers. Never run AOV angles to cold traffic; nobody buys a $59.99 bundle from a brand
  they have never heard of, at first sight.
- **Objective:** Sales, optimise for average order value.
- **Success metric:** AOV lift over your baseline single-item AOV, cost per purchase under ~$22.
- **Why test it:** bundles are the difference between a $26 average order and a $45 one, which is the
  difference between losing money and making it with paid traffic.

### Budget discipline when you do start

Start at **$10/day per ad set, one ad set per angle, for 4 days minimum** before judging anything. Do not
touch the budget in the first 72 hours — early data is noise. Kill an ad only if it has spent 2× the target
CPA with zero purchases. Expect the first real signal around **$150–250 of spend per angle**; anything
below that is not a result, it is a coin flip.

About Sniffari

We sell the small, unglamorous things that make living with an indoor pet easier.

Most pet stores sell you the fun stuff: a toy shaped like a taco, a bed in the shape of a donut. Meanwhile the two things that actually change daily life — a bored animal and a house covered in fur — get nothing.

Sniffari stocks only the unglamorous jobs:

  • Boredom. Snuffle mats, lick mats and slow feeders that turn a meal into a job.
  • Fur. A roller, a slicker brush and a glove that between them handle almost all shedding.
  • The outside coming inside. A paw cleaner cup and a leakproof walk bottle.

How we choose products

  1. It solves something specific. If we cannot describe the problem in one sentence, it does not go in.
  2. It demos in fifteen seconds. If it is not obvious on camera, we cannot show you why it is worth buying.
  3. It costs 3× less than it sells for. Otherwise shipping and returns eat the business and the price goes up.
  4. It is small, light and hard to break. Nothing fragile, nothing heavy, nothing with a battery that can fail.
  5. It has real supplier feedback. We print the supplier's rating and units sold on the product page, so you see what we saw.

What we refuse to do

  • No fake reviews. Not one. The review section says so out loud until real reviews exist.
  • No fake urgency. No countdown timers, no “only 3 left”, no “17 people are looking at this”.
  • No health claims. Nothing here treats, cures or prevents anything.
  • No invented shipping times. We quote 7–15 days because that is what the carriers actually do.
  • No branded or knock-off goods. Everything is unbranded, generic and legally clean.

How the money works

We do not hold stock. When you order, our supplier partner packs and ships your item directly, which is why delivery takes 7–15 days instead of two, and why prices are what they are. It also means we only make money if the product is good enough that you do not return it — which is a decent incentive.

Where we are

Sniffari is a small independent store. Questions, problems, or a product you think we should stock: contact us — a human answers, usually within one business day.
